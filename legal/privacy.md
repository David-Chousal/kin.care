# Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

## 1. Who we are

Kin (“we”, “us”) provides a family care coordination app (“Kin”) that helps caregivers track tasks, medications, health logs, appointments, check-ins, and related documents.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect:

- Account information (such as name and email address)
- Care-related information you enter (tasks, medications, health logs, calendar events, check-ins)
- Files you upload (for example medical, legal, or insurance documents)
- Photos you attach to health logs (if you choose to attach them)
- Push notification token(s) (if you enable notifications)
- Basic diagnostics data (see “Diagnostics” below)

Health logs and medication records may contain sensitive health information.

## 3. How we use information

We use your information to:

- Provide Kin and sync data within your family group
- Send reminders and notifications you request
- Support AI features like Visit Prep summaries and drug interaction checks
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your health information for advertising.

## 4. AI processing

If you use AI features, we send relevant inputs you provide (for example, medications and recent health logs) to an AI model provider to generate an output.

As of this policy’s “Last updated” date, Kin uses Groq as an AI model provider for these features.

We may store limited metadata about AI requests to support reliability, rate limiting, cost controls, and abuse prevention. This can include: request purpose; timestamps; approximate request and response sizes; provider-reported token counts when available; latency; a best-effort client network identifier (for example the first IP in a forwarded header, which may be inaccurate); and an internal cryptographic hash of the prompt (not the prompt text itself).

## 5. Storage and security (Supabase)

Kin uses Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. Access to family data is restricted to members of that family group, and enforced using database row-level security policies.

## 6. Push notifications (Expo)

If you enable notifications, Kin stores your device push notification token and uses it to send reminders and task-related alerts. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

## 7. Diagnostics (Sentry)

We use Sentry to diagnose crashes and performance issues. Diagnostic data may include device and app information (such as OS version, device model, app version) and crash details. Kin does not attach full-screen screenshots to Sentry crash reports by default.

We strive to avoid sending sensitive health content to Sentry; however, depending on the nature of an error, some user-entered text could be included in logs if it is part of the failure context.

## 8. Sharing with third parties

We share information with service providers who help us operate Kin, such as Supabase (auth, database, storage), Expo (push notifications), an AI model provider (currently Groq), and Sentry (diagnostics). These providers process data only as needed to provide their services to us.

We do not share your health information with insurers, advertisers, or data brokers.

## 9. Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data is removed within 30 days. Family data shared with other family members may be retained by those members’ accounts.

## 10. Your choices and rights

You can export family data in Settings. You can request account deletion using in-app options or by contacting us.

## 11. Children’s privacy

Kin is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Continued use of Kin after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests: privacy@kin.care
